Privacy Policy – Habesha Hub

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Habesha Hub ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and your rights when you use the Habesha Hub mobile app and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name and nickname when you register or sign in with Google or Apple. If you use the app without an account, we create an anonymous ID so features like chat and favorites work.
  • Business information: name, address, phone number, photos, videos, menu and opening hours if you register a business. This is shown publicly in the app.
  • Marketplace listings: title, description, price, city, photos and the phone number you choose to show. Listings are public.
  • Bookings and receipts: the service you book, date, notes, the country you pay from, the agreed amount, payment method and whether the booking is paid.
  • Messages: chat messages sent to businesses in the app, reviews you post, and messages sent through the contact form.
  • AI assistant: the questions you type in the AI chat.
  • Notification token: a device token so we can send you push notifications (for example when a booking is confirmed).
  • Location: your GPS position, only at the moment you search for nearby businesses. It is not stored.
  • On your device only: favorite businesses, language and theme settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To run Habesha Hub: show businesses, services, marketplace listings and videos.
  • To handle bookings: confirm your booking, show your receipt and tell you how to pay.
  • To send push notifications about your bookings and payments.
  • To answer your questions in the AI assistant.
  • To answer messages you send us, and to prevent misuse.

3. Payments

Habesha Hub does not process card payments and never stores card or bank account numbers. After a booking is confirmed, you pay directly with a payment app or service you choose (for example Vipps, Swish, MobilePay, PayPal, telebirr, M-Pesa or Mobile Money). Those services have their own privacy policies. We only store the amount, method, reference and whether the payment was received.

5. Location Data

We access your location only when you tap the search button to find nearby businesses. We do not track or store your location.

6. Children's Privacy

Habesha Hub is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, and to object to processing. You can delete your account in the app under Profile, or contact us and we will delete your data.

8. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Booking records and receipts may be kept for up to 5 years when required for accounting. Contact messages are deleted when they are no longer needed.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is limited by Firebase security rules, and API keys are kept on our servers, not in the app.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
