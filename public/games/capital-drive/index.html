Race the computer or real people in four capitals. Computer: arrow keys or WASD, Shift for boost, H for the horn. 🎵 turns the Habesha music on or off. Phone: the round buttons. Switch the language at the top: English, Tigrinya or Amharic. Online races: quick races match whoever is online in the same city; friends races use a shared code. Kampala drives on the left, like in real life.
