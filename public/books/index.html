About the author

Yossief Werke Asrat is a teacher living in Sweden. He writes books that help Habesha children all over the world learn to read and write Tigrinya.

Metsihaf Eyo Tigrinya

Emkulu Publisher · 2022 · 197 pages · A4

A reading and writing workbook. The Fidel letters are grouped by shape and logic so children learn faster, with step-by-step exercises from single words to full sentences. For children from early school age up to teenagers.
