🚦 Practical driving test

Drive a real 3D town with a driving instructor who speaks Tigrinya, Amharic, English or Norwegian. Traffic lights, STOP, give way, zebra crossings, speed limits, GPS and parking. Get “ኣንበሳ!” when you drive well and “ኣታ ቀስ በል!” when you speed.

  1. Open Games & Tools › Habesha Games › 🚦 Driving test
  2. Choose where to drive (hospital, school, church…) and the instructor language
  3. Press Start. Steer with the wheel, gas and brake on the right, D/R for reverse
  4. Follow the voice and the GPS, then stop inside the P bay to finish
ብትግርኛ

📘 Driving theory — 500 questions

Norwegian class B theory in Tigrinya with road signs: practice rules, practice signs and a real exam. 20 + 20 + 20 questions are free.

  1. Open Games & Tools › Life Tools › Driving licence
  2. Practice rules, signs, or take the exam
  3. For the full course and the practical test: write help on WhatsApp to +47 973 74 482 to get your personal code
  4. Tap Unlock with code and type it in

🏎️ Capital Racing 3D

Race through Asmara, Addis Ababa, Kampala and Oslo against the computer or real people online. Taxi mode, crashes, and a countdown in Tigrinya.

  1. Open Habesha Games › Capital Racing 3D
  2. Choose a city and race the computer or go online
  3. Turn the phone sideways and tap the game to hide the top bar

🎲 Habesha games

Dama with a friend or anyone online, Genna (the Christmas stick game) and riddles in your language.

  1. Open Habesha Games and pick a game
  2. Dama: play the computer, create a code for a friend, or play a random player
  3. Choose EN, ትግ or አማ at the top: all texts follow

👶 Fidel for kids

Children tap a letter family, see and hear every sound, then play the quiz to earn stars and go up levels.

  1. Open Habesha Games › Fidel
  2. Learn: tap ሀ ለ ሐ… and each of the 7 sounds
  3. Quiz: pick the right sound — 5 stars = next level

💬 Questions

Frequently asked

Is Habesha Hub free?

Yes. The app is 100% free for everyone. Businesses can register for free too — Premium is optional.

Which countries does it work in?

Everywhere. Today there are 1,200+ Habesha places in more than 50 countries, and new ones are added every week. You can also suggest a place that is missing.

Do I need an account?

No. You can search, explore and use the map without logging in. An account is only needed to book, sell or register a business.

My business is missing or wrong — what do I do?

Open it in the app and tap “Is this your business?” to claim it, or “Report wrong info”. Missing completely? Use “Suggest a business” in Explore.

Which languages are supported?

English, Tigrinya (ትግርኛ) and Amharic (አማርኛ). You can switch any time in the app.
